# BijliBhai

**Bijli ka kaam? Bhai ko bulao.**

BijliBhai is a local electrician home-service brand for **Kanpur, Shuklaganj and Unnao**, Uttar Pradesh. We help households get a trusted local electrician at the door for everyday electrical work — without an app, login, or complicated booking flow.

Customers simply **call or WhatsApp**. That is the booking system for this launch.

---

## What we do

BijliBhai coordinates doorstep electrician service for homes:

- Switch and socket repair / fitting
- Fan installation and repair
- Light installation
- MCB, fuse, and DB work
- Wiring and new power points
- Inverter connection and check
- Electrical inspection / visit
- Urgent electrical help (sparking, burning smell, sudden power issues)

We show **starting labour prices** on the website. Parts and material are usually extra. Expected labour and material cost is shared with the customer **before work starts**.

We do not claim guaranteed arrival times, police verification, or city-wide coverage.
